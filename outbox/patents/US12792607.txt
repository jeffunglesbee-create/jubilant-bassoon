Title: Systems and methods for managing storage system monitoring data using value detection
Grant date: 2026-11-10
Assignee: NetApp, Inc.
Inventors: Carlos Rolo
Source: ppubs
PPUBS GUID: US-12792607-B2

======================================================================
CLAIMS
======================================================================

Claim 1 [independent]:
A storage system monitoring method, comprising: obtaining a segment of storage system monitoring data; generating a compressed segment from the segment and a reconstructed segment from the compressed segment; identifying locations in the reconstructed segment based on a comparison of the segment and the reconstructed segment, wherein the identified locations comprise anomalous values in the reconstructed segment; determining original values for the identified locations, wherein the original values are associated with indices in the reconstructed segment mapped to segment times; receiving a query indicating a portion of the storage system monitoring data; in response to the query, retrieving the identified locations, the original values and the compressed segment; and reconstructing the portion using the original values to replace the anomalous values at the identified locations.

Claim 2 [dep on 1]:
The method of claim 1, wherein: the locations are identified based on a difference between: the original values, first derivative, or second derivative of the segment and corresponding original values, first derivative, or second derivative of the reconstructed segment.

Claim 3 [dep on 2]:
The method of claim 2, wherein: the locations are identified based on a position-dependent threshold.

Claim 4 [dep on 1]:
The method of claim 1, wherein: the original values for the identified locations are values of the segment at the locations, or values for the identified locations are based on the values of the segment at the locations and corresponding values of the reconstructed segment at the locations.

Claim 5 [dep on 1]:
The method of claim 1, wherein: the compressed segment is generated using a lossy compression method.

Claim 6 [dep on 1]:
The method of claim 1, wherein: the compressed segment is generated using at least one of polynomial approximation, frequency domain transformation, or audio encoding.

Claim 7 [dep on 1]:
The method of claim 1, wherein: the storage system monitoring data includes CPU I/O wait time, CPU Guest Usage, CPU usage, System Status, number of connected clients, network usage, memory usage, disk usage, read latency, write latency, or operating system load.

Claim 8 [dep on 1]:
The method of claim 1, wherein: identifying the locations occurs during the generation of the compressed segment.
