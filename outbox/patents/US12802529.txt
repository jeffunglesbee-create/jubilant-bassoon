CLAIMS
======================================================================

Claim 1 [independent]:
A single game parlay system comprising: a user device having a processor executing first non-transient computer instructions which instantiate a single game parlay (“SGP”) application; wherein the SGP application provides an SGP application user interface which facilitates selection, by a user of the user device, of an SGP bet for an SGP line for a combination of activities for an event;  a front-end server having a processor executing second non-transient computer instructions which instantiate an event simulation engine (“ESE”), a Props counter engine (“PCE”), and an SGP pricing engine (“SPE”); and a back-end server communicating, to the front-end server, real-time betting data for the combination of activities for the event; and wherein the ESE generates two or more simulated results for the combination of activities based upon the real-time betting data received from the back-end server.

Claim 2 [dep on 1]:
The single game parlay system of claim 1, wherein the back-end server communicates the betting data to the front-end server on a substantially real-time basis while one or more activities of the combination of activities are occurring; and wherein the front-end server, on a substantially real-time basis and based on the betting data received from the back-end server, performs operations comprising: determining a first probability of a future result occurring for the combination of activities; associating a first pricing with the first probability; determining a second probability of a future result not occurring for the combination of activities; associating a second pricing with the second probability; generating an SGP line based on the first pricing and the second pricing; and communicating the SGP line to the SGP application instantiated on the user device; and  wherein the SGP application user interface presents the SGP line to the user.

Claim 3 [dep on 2]:
The single game parlay system of claim 2, wherein the ESE performs ESE operations comprising: generating simulations of results for each activity of the combination of activities for the event; and outputting the simulations to the PCE; and  wherein the PCE performs PCE operations comprising: determining the first probability and the second probability based on the simulations received from the ESE.

Claim 4 [dep on 3]:
The single game parlay system of claim 3, wherein at least three simulations are generated by the ESE and are utilized by the PCE to determine at least one of the first probability and the second probability.

Claim 5 [dep on 3]:
The single game parlay system of claim 3, wherein the PCE operations further comprise: updating, based on the real-time betting data received from the back-end server, a counter.

Claim 6 [dep on 5]:
The single game parlay system of claim 5, wherein the counter is an array indicating, on a substantially real-time basis, contributions by a given participant, associated with a given player, toward a given result for at least one activity of the combination of activities for the event.

Claim 7 [dep on 6]:
The single game parlay system of claim 6, wherein the PCE operations further comprise: generating a bet selection for the given participant based on the counter, the first probability and the second probability; and outputting the bet selection to the SPE.

Claim 8 [dep on 7]:
The single game parlay system of claim 7, wherein the SPE performs SPE operations comprising: determining pricing for the SGP line based on the bet selection.

Claim 9 [dep on 8]:
The single game parlay system of claim 8, wherein the SGP line includes, as a first activity of the combination of activities, a participant scoring a certain number of points and, as a second activity of the combination of activities, a participant winning an event; wherein the SGP line includes odds for the first activity and the second activity occurring during the event; and wherein the SGP line includes second odds for the first activity and the second activity not occurring during the event.

Claim 10 [dep on 2]:
The single game parlay system of claim 2, wherein the front-end server determines on a substantially real-time basis and based on the real-time betting data received, on a substantially real-time basis, from the back-end server, an updated betting option for the SGP bet; wherein the front-end server communicates, on a substantially real-time basis, the updated betting option for the SGP bet to the SGP application instantiated on the user device; and wherein the SGP application presents the updated betting option for the SGP bet as an updated SGP bet to the user in order to facilitate substantially real-time selection by the user of the updated SGP bet during the event.

Claim 11 [dep on 10]:
The single game parlay system of claim 10, wherein the updated betting option is generated based on a counter determined by the front-end server based on real-time performance metrics associated with at least one participant in at least one of the combination of activities for the event.

Claim 12 [dep on 11]:
The single game parlay system of claim 11, wherein the updated betting option is further generated based on at least three simulated results, further determined by the front-end server based on historical results, for at least one of the combination of activities for the event.

Claim 13 [dep on 12]:
The single game parlay system of claim 12, wherein the updated betting option is further generated based on a real-time simulated result, determined by the front-end server based on the real-time betting data received from the back-end server, for the at least one of the combination of activities for the event.

Claim 14 [dep on 1]:
The single game parlay system of claim 1, wherein the combination of activities for the event includes at least one of a team activity for an event and a participant performance metric for the event.

Claim 15 [dep on 1]:
The single game parlay system of claim 1, wherein the SGP line includes a selection of two or more betting activity options, for a given event, selected on a predefined basis by an operator of the front-end server.

Claim 16 [dep on 1]:
The single game parlay system of claim 1, wherein the SGP line includes a combination, identified by the user, of two or more betting activity options for a given event.

Claim 17 [independent]:
A method, for determining a bet selection for a single game parlay (SGP), comprising operations including: simulating, by an event simulation engine (ESE) instantiated by a front-end server (FES), two combined activities for an event to determine simulated combined activity results; first determining, by the ESE based on real-time betting data, actual results for a participant in the at least one activity of the two combined activities for the event; wherein a back-end server, coupled to the FES, communicates the real-time betting data to the FES on a substantially real-time basis while at least one activity of the two combined activities are occurring;  allocating, by a prop counter engine (PCE) instantiated by the FES, the simulated combined activity results to the participant based on the actual results for the participant; generating, by the PCE, based on a result of the allocating, allocated participant results; second determining, by the PCE, a probability of the participant achieving a future result in at least one of the two combined activities for the event based on the allocated participant results; third determining, by a SGP pricing engine (SPE) instantiated by the FES, a pricing for the future result; and second generating, by the SPE, an SGP line providing odds and returns for the participant achieving and not achieving the future result.

Claim 18 [dep on 17]:
The method of claim 17, further comprising: iteratively simulating the two combined activities, based on the real-time betting data, for one of the two combined activities to determine updated simulated combined activity results; and further performing the first determining, allocating, generating, second determining, third determining and second generating operations based on the real-time betting data.

Claim 19 [independent]:
A computer readable medium storing non-transient computer instructions which, when executed by a processor in a front-end server (FES) for an online gaming system, instruct the FES to perform operations comprising: simulating, by an event simulation engine (ESE) instantiated by the processor, at least two combined activities for an event to determine simulated combined activity results; first determining, by the ESE and based on real-time betting data, actual results for a participant in at least one activity of the at least two combined activities for the event; wherein a back-end server coupled to the FES communicates the real-time betting data to the FES on a substantially real-time basis while at least one activity of the at least two combined activities are occurring;  allocating, by a props counter engine (PCE) instantiated by the FES, the simulated combined activity results to the participant based on the actual results for the participant; generating, by the PCE, based on a result of the allocating, allocated participant results; second determining, by the PCE, a probability of the participant achieving a future result in at least one of the at least two combined activities for the event based on the allocated participant results; third determining, by a same game parlay pricing engine (SPE) instantiated by the FES, a pricing for the future result; and second generating, by the SPE, a single game parlay (SGP) line providing odds and returns for the participant achieving and not achieving the future result.

Claim 20 [dep on 19]:
The computer readable medium of claim 19, wherein the SGP line includes, in the at least two combined activities for the event, user selected activities.
