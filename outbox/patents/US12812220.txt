Title: Systems, apparatus, and methods for providing event video streams and synchronized event information via multiple internet channels

CLAIMS

Claim 1 [independent]:
A system, comprising: at least one media source to: receive an audio/video feed of a live event; provide a first copy of the audio/video feed of the live event from a first media server endpoint of the at least one media source to a first client device that includes at least one first display; receive from the first client device a composite outgoing stream of the audio/video feed and additional video content; and provide a first copy of the composite outgoing stream of the audio/video feed and additional video content from a second media server endpoint of the at least one media source; and  a webserver to transmit first instructions to a second client device that includes at least one second display to cause the second client device to: connect to the second media server endpoint of the at least one media source to receive, on a first communication channel between the second media server endpoint and the second client device, the first copy of the composite outgoing stream; and connect to a socket of a socket server to receive, on a second communication channel between the socket and the second client device, first digital content corresponding to first event information relating to the live event.

Claim 2 [dep on 1]:
The system of claim 1, wherein: the at least one media source comprises at least one of: at least one WebRTC media server; or at least one RTMP media server; and  the at least one of the at least one WebRTC media server or the at least one RTMP media server is configured to: receive the audio/video feed of the live event; provide the first copy of the audio/video feed of the live event as a WebRTC copy or an RTMP copy from the first media server endpoint of the at least one media source to the first client device; and receive from the first client device the composite outgoing stream of the audio/video feed and the additional video content as a WebRTC composite outgoing stream or an RTMP outgoing stream.

Claim 3 [dep on 2]:
The system of claim 2, wherein: the at least one media source further comprises at least one of: an RTMP CDN; or at least one HLS server; and  the at least one media source is configured to provide the first copy of the composite outgoing stream of the audio/video feed and the additional video content from the second media server endpoint of the at least one media source as at least one of: a first WebRTC copy of the composite outgoing stream; a first RTMP copy of the composite outgoing stream; a first RTMP CDN copy of the composite outgoing stream; or a first HLS copy of the composite outgoing stream.

Claim 4 [dep on 1]:
The system of claim 1, wherein: the at least one media source comprises at least one RTMP media server and at least one HLS server; the at least one RTMP media server is configured to receive the audio/video feed of the live event and transmit a first HLS copy of the audio/video feed of the live event from the at least one RTMP media server to the at least one HLS server; and the at least one media source is configured to provide the first copy of the audio/video feed of the live event as another HLS copy from the first media server endpoint of the at least one HLS server to the first client device.

Claim 5 [dep on 1]:
The system of claim 1, wherein: the live event is a sporting or gaming event; and the first event information is online gaming information.

Claim 6 [dep on 1]:
The system of claim 1, wherein: the live event is a sporting or gaming event; and the first event information is score information.

Claim 7 [dep on 1]:
The system of claim 1, further comprising the socket server.

Claim 8 [dep on 7]:
The system of claim 7, further comprising: a control server, coupled to the socket server, to provide the first digital content corresponding to the first event information to the socket server.

Claim 9 [dep on 1]:
The system of claim 1, wherein the webserver is further configured to transmit second instructions to the first client device to cause the first client device to: connect to the socket of the socket server to receive, on a third communication channel between the socket and the first client device, the first digital content corresponding to the first event information.

Claim 10 [dep on 1]:
The system of claim 1, wherein the webserver is configured to transmit third instructions to a third client device that includes at least one third display to enable the at least one third display of the third client device to render the first event information relating to the live event, wherein the third instructions transmitted by the webserver cause the third client device to: connect to the socket of the socket server to receive, on a fourth communication channel between the socket and the third client device, the first digital content corresponding to the first event information.

Claim 11 [dep on 10]:
The system of claim 10, wherein the webserver is configured to transmit the third instructions to the third client device to further enable the at least one third display of the third client device to render the first video relating to the live event and render the first event information relating to the live event, wherein the third instructions transmitted by the webserver cause the third client device to: connect to the second media server endpoint to receive, on a fifth communication channel between the second media server endpoint and the third client device, the first copy of the composite outgoing stream.

Claim 12 [dep on 11]:
The system of claim 11, wherein: the third instructions transmitted by the webserver further cause the third client device to: render, on the at least one third display of the third client device, the first video relating to the live event based on the first copy of the composite outgoing stream received on the fifth communication channel; and render, on the at least one third display of the third client device, at least some of the first event information based on the first digital content received on the fourth communication channel.

Claim 13 [dep on 12]:
The system of claim 12, wherein: the first instructions transmitted by the webserver cause the second client device to: render, on the at least one second display of the second client device, at least some of the first event information based on the first digital content received on the second communication channel; and obtain access to first additional information relating to the live event and/or redirect to a first web site or first app when a first user selects at least a portion of the rendered at least some of the first event information; and  the third instructions transmitted by the webserver cause the third client device to obtain access to the first additional information or second additional information relating to the live event and/or redirect to the first web site, the first app, a second web site or a second app related to the live event when a second user selects at least a portion of the rendered at least some of the first event information.
